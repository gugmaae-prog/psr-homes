# Dubai South rental-demand and project-proximity research

Evidence date: 6 September 2026  
Use: PSR advisor research, client briefs and AI grounding  
Scope: Dubai South Residential District, Emaar South, Expo Living / Expo City and the selected southern-corridor projects in the Jumanah brief

## Decision summary

Dubai South already has an operating residential and employment base; it is not only a future-infrastructure story. The Residential District reported more than 30,000 residents, operating daily-life amenities, GEMS Founders School and bus access to Expo Metro. Dubai South reported 4,044 operating companies after adding 415 in 2024, while Business Park office leasing reached 500,000 sqft. Those facts support current tenant-demand channels from aviation, logistics, business services, Expo City and families.

The AED 128 billion Al Maktoum passenger-terminal programme and the AED 10 billion Dubai Exhibition Centre expansion are important forward catalysts, but neither should be capitalised into today's rent without a conservative unit-level model. The airport programme targets ultimate capacity of 260 million passengers. The exhibition-centre plan targets 180,000 sqm by 2031. These are phased programmes, not current throughput or guaranteed tenant growth.

## Evidence hierarchy used by PSR

1. Developer and government sources establish project status, operating infrastructure and announced programmes.
2. DLD Rental Index, registered Ejari evidence and executed leases establish the strongest rental evidence.
3. Property Finder, Dubizzle and Bayut establish current asking-market signals only. Their counts may include duplicate, stale or already-let advertisements.
4. PSR `/map` coordinates support straight-line screening. They do not replace an exact site pin, road routing or peak-hour drive-time check.
5. Advisor-entered prices, rents, fees and occupancy assumptions remain dated scenarios until verified against the exact unit and documents.

## Likely renter-demand segments

| Segment | Likely unit fit | Evidence and limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Aviation, aerospace and airport-services professionals | Studios and 1BRs; selected 2BRs for established households | Current aviation and aerospace operations support the channel. Future airport jobs are not treated as current tenants. |
| Logistics, freight, e-commerce and trade employees | Value-led studios and 1BRs; practical 2BRs for families or sharing | Dubai South cites operating FedEx, dnata and healthcare-logistics facilities. No public dataset states the residential tenure share of this workforce. |
| Expo City, exhibitions and event-economy professionals | Furnished studios and 1BRs; 2BRs for longer assignments | Expo City has an operating business and visitor ecosystem. Exhibition-centre demand should be phased with delivered capacity. |
| SME founders, consultants and business-park employees | Studios and 1BRs; selected 2BRs | Company and office-leasing growth support a professional pool, but do not prove where each employee lives. |
| Families anchored by schools and daily-life services | Larger 1BRs, 2BRs and 3BRs with parking and child-oriented amenities | The Residential District reports an operating school, parks, retail and more than 30,000 residents. Family demand is an inference, not a measured demographic share. |
| Value-conscious commuters and frequent travellers | Efficient studios and 1BRs near road, bus or Metro connections | Bus-to-Expo-Metro and airport access support the proposition. The exact building and peak-hour route decide whether it works in practice. |

No official public dataset found in this review identifies Dubai South tenants by nationality, income, occupation, household size or bedroom preference at building level. PSR must describe these as likely segments, never as demographic percentages.

## Map and amenity QA

The live PSR map snapshot exported on 6 September 2026 contained 1,335 geocoded project records, 215 communities and 10,841 amenity records. It is useful for broad screening, but it also contains community-centroid fallbacks and some misclassified or suspect records. The report therefore uses manually reviewed project pins and official establishments for client-facing claims.

Examples retained after review include GEMS Founders School Dubai South, Saudi German Clinic South Village, Expo 2020 Metro Station and Al Maktoum International Airport. Generic Overture place records such as similarly named malls, accommodation entries or misclassified cultural places are not automatically promoted into client reports.
