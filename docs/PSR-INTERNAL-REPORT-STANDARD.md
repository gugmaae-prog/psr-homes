# PSR internal property-report standard

This standard is enforced by `worker/psr-report-standards.ts`, the agent narrative prompt and the curated PDF renderer.

## Voice

- Write as PSR Homes using `we`, `our` and `PSR Homes`.
- Do not use first-person singular language in client-facing copy.
- Use the heading `Recommendations` and state the routes directly.
- Keep the tone objective, professional and decision-led.

## Evidence and volatility

- Separate official sources, developer material, portal asking evidence, PSR calculations and advisor-entered assumptions.
- Prices, availability, inventory, incentives, fees, service charges, payment schedules, completion dates, rental assumptions and market data may change.
- Confirm the exact unit, current inventory, booking form, payment ledger, DLD or Oqood position, SPA, unit plan, parking, handover charges and recurring costs in writing before client issue or reservation.
- Omit a client-facing section when its only conclusion is that data is unavailable. Never infer buyer demographics from enquiries or broad market statistics.

## Return presentation

- Rental ROI is shown only for completed or ready properties that can be assessed against current, configuration-matched leasing evidence.
- Gross rental ROI = annual rent / unit price.
- Occupancy-adjusted net ROI = `(annual rent x occupancy - annual service charge - other recurring costs) / all-in acquisition basis`.
- Present base and upper rental sensitivities only when both rent inputs are stored.
- Label the upper case as an illustrative sensitivity, not a forecast or guarantee.
- Do not include assumed capital appreciation in rental ROI.
- Do not publish rental ROI for off-plan projects. Compare off-plan options using unit price, AED per sqft, payment timing, DLD or Oqood treatment, handover evidence, documentation and total acquisition exposure.
- Source-dated infrastructure and district programmes may be described as demand catalysts; do not state that they cause a specific rent, yield or capital gain.

## DLD incentives

- Cost the standard DLD or Oqood amount unless the exact unit's current developer booking form, reservation form or written sales confirmation applies a waiver or discount.
- A current portal or broker listing may be shown as a marketed offer check, with its source date and unit or payment-plan limitation, but it must not be deducted from the all-in cost before developer confirmation.
- Do not carry a project-level waiver across phases, unit types, cash offers or booking dates.

## Required project module

Each selected project should contain the project and developer identity, emirate and community, exact-unit price and AED per sqft, unit area, payment schedule, one-time acquisition and handover costs, annual ownership costs, completion evidence, project gallery and available floor plans. Completed or ready options additionally require current rental sensitivities and occupancy-adjusted return. Off-plan options require DLD treatment, payment timing and handover-document checks without rental ROI. Missing material inputs remain verification items in the unit record, not marketing claims.

## Presentation

- Use the PSR sans-serif type system for titles and body copy.
- Use a source-appropriate location image on the cover. The Jumanah Dubai South client brief uses the official Expo City Al Wasl Dome image.
- An advisor portrait may appear on the cover and advisor page; the crop must remain a professional head-and-shoulders view.
- Preserve clear source and methodology pages, document dates and variability disclosures.
